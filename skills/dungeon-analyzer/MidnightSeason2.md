# Midnight Season 2 Mythic+ dungeons

The Midnight Season 2 Mythic+ pool as listed on
<https://www.method.gg/guides#mythic-plus-guides>. Match a report's `fights.game_zone_id` to
the **Ids** line of a dungeon below (local logs also carry `fights.challenge_mode_id`).

## Altar of Fangs

- **Description:** A Midnight dungeon held by a serpent-worshipping troll cult. Venom and
  poison dominate: trash evolves and empowers serpents, and the bosses stack venom effects
  the group has to clear.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 2993, `challenge_mode_id` 588. Timer 30:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - Rav'i
  - The Writhing Coil
  - Zul'jan
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/altar-of-fangs>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/altar-of-fangs/ability-tracker>

## Den of Nalorakk

- **Description:** A Midnight dungeon in the den of Nalorakk, the Amani bear loa. Expect
  spirit-themed trash, an icy arena where players slide, and a final boss that leaves
  spectral echoes the group has to manage.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 2825, `challenge_mode_id` 586. Timer 32:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - The Hoardmonger
  - Sentinel of Winter
  - Nalorakk
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/den-of-nalorakk>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/den-of-nalorakk/ability-tracker>

## Kings' Rest

- **Description:** A Battle for Azeroth dungeon: the Zandalari royal tomb in Zuldazar.
  Animated constructs and undead guardians fill the trash, and the encounters include a
  council of three tribal champions and the first Zandalari king.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 1762, `challenge_mode_id` 249. Timer 33:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - The Golden Serpent
  - Mchimba the Embalmer
  - The Council of Tribes (Kula the Butcher, Aka'ali the Conqueror, Zanazal the Wise)
  - Dazar, the First King (with Reban and T'zala)
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/kings-rest>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/kings-rest/ability-tracker>

## Murder Row

- **Description:** A Midnight dungeon in a criminal underworld overrun by the Burning Legion.
  Trash is demons and corrupted warlocks with dangerous casts and curses, and the bosses are
  fel-empowered crime lords and a Legion annihilator.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 2813, `challenge_mode_id` 587. Timer 34:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - Kystia Manaheart (with Nibbles)
  - Zaen Bladesorrow
  - Xathuux the Annihilator
  - Lithiel Cinderfury
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/murder-row>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/murder-row/ability-tracker>

## Ruby Life Pools

- **Description:** A Dragonflight dungeon in the Waking Shores, where primalists attack the
  red dragonflight's life pools. Trash and bosses mix ice, fire, and storm elements, ending in
  a two-boss fight against a proto-drake and its rider.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 2521, `challenge_mode_id` 399. Timer 28:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - Melidrussa Chillworn
  - Kokia Blazehoof
  - Kyrakka and Erkhart Stormvein
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/ruby-life-pools>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/ruby-life-pools/ability-tracker>

## Temple of Sethraliss

- **Description:** A Battle for Azeroth dungeon in Vol'dun: the temple of the serpent loa
  Sethraliss. Lightning-themed bosses and krolusk-riding trash lead to a final encounter
  where the group protects and heals the loa's avatar while killing corrupted adds.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 1877, `challenge_mode_id` 250. Timer 32:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - Adderis and Aspix
  - Merektha
  - Galvazzt
  - Avatar of Sethraliss
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/temple-of-sethraliss>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/temple-of-sethraliss/ability-tracker>

## The Blinding Vale

- **Description:** A Midnight dungeon in an overgrown, light-infused wilderness. Trash is
  plants, spore beasts, and light casters, and the bosses lean on soaks, ground hazards, and
  add waves, including a druid boss that shifts forms as its health drops.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 2859, `challenge_mode_id` 584. Timer 30:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - Lightblossom Trinity (three bosses, including Kezkitt and Lekshi)
  - Ikuzz the Light Hunter
  - Lightwarden Ruia
  - Ziekket
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/the-blinding-vale>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/the-blinding-vale/ability-tracker>

## Voidscar Arena

- **Description:** A Midnight dungeon set in a void-touched gladiatorial arena. Trash is
  pit fighters, overseers, and void casters, and the three bosses test spreading, puddle
  placement, and avoiding void orbs.
- **Ids:** `game_zone_id` 2923, `challenge_mode_id` 585. Timer 30:00.
- **Bosses:**
  - Taz'Rah
  - Atroxus
  - Charonus
- **Guide:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/voidscar-arena>
- **Ability tracker:** <https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/voidscar-arena/ability-tracker>
